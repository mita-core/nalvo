Billing details

No mystery line items

What counts as usage?

Model usage, outbound connection calls, artifact storage and egress. Each charge stays attached to its execution and worker, so the expensive stage is visible immediately.

What happens at the spend cap?

Queued executions stop opening and active work finishes its current stage before parking. Raise the boundary to resume; an 80% notification arrives before the stop.

Do viewers take a seat?

No. Read-only access is free and unlimited. A seat is used only by people who can start or pause work, change policy or resolve checkpoints.

Can we switch plans mid-month?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next renewal so you keep what you paid for. Annual plans can add seats at any time.

Is there a trial on Scale?

Fourteen days with the full Scale feature set, no card. If you do nothing at the end, the workspace drops to Starter rather than being deleted.
