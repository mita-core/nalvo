Practical details

What teams need to know

Does adoption change our execution code?

No. Nalvo observes the framework, custom loop or scheduled script you already run. Register a worker and emit its stages through the SDK or API; most teams capture their first complete record in one afternoon.

How are consequential actions held for review?

Define checkpoints around spend, data classes or specific connections. The execution parks when a rule matches, the reviewer receives the change and its context, and work continues or ends from that recorded decision.

Where does our data live?

Choose an EU or US region. Payloads can be redacted before inference, artifacts expire on your retention schedule, and the evidence record can preserve the decision without retaining the sensitive body.

How does pricing work?

Each workspace pays a fixed subscription plus measured compute, connection calls and storage. The full breakdown is on the pricing page, and every tier includes a hard spending boundary.

Can we self-host?

The evidence service is hosted. Enterprise customers can place execution relays inside their own VPC so payloads stay on-network, while policy definitions and signed exports remain portable.
