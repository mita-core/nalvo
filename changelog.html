Changelog

What shipped

Weekly releases, no maintenance windows. Breaking API changes get a version bump and six months of overlap.

Aug 1, 2024
v2.8
NewCheckpoints

Risk tiers and timed escalation

Checkpoint requests now carry a risk score and route to different groups by tier. If nobody answers inside the SLA, the request escalates instead of waiting unseen.

  • Three configurable risk tiers per workspace
  • Auto-escalation with a per-tier timeout
  • Evidence attached to every checkpoint, visible in the context panel
  • Checkpoint policy is versioned alongside the worker definition
Jul 24, 2024
v2.7
NewCostFixes

Cost attribution per stage

Spend is now broken down by stage, not just per execution, so an expensive summarisation call is obvious at a glance. Routing suggestions appear when a cheaper model would have done.

  • Per-stage token and connection cost in execution detail
  • Monthly cap with a hard stop and an 80% alert
  • Fixed: cost forecast ignored partially completed executions
  • Fixed: egress was double-counted for S3 artifacts
Jul 11, 2024
v2.6
NewConsole

Resizable, collapsible panels

The navigation and context panels can be dragged, collapsed and restored. Collapsed panels leave a floating bar so nothing becomes unreachable, and double-clicking a gutter snaps it back to the default width.

  • Drag-to-resize with min and max bounds
  • Floating quick-nav while the sidebar is collapsed
  • Execution history loads all 2,847 rows — pagination removed
Jun 28, 2024
v2.5
NewSecurity

SCIM provisioning and enforced two-factor

Members can be provisioned and deprovisioned from your identity provider. Workspaces can require a second factor for everyone, with admin-side reset from the team page.

  • SCIM 2.0 with group-to-role mapping
  • Workspace-wide two-factor enforcement
  • Session timeout and IP allow-list settings
Jun 6, 2024
v2.4
ConnectionsFixes

Connection registry with scopes

Connections are registered once with a JSON schema and granted per worker. Deprecated connections keep working until their removal date and warn on every call.

  • Per-worker connection grants with a pending-request state
  • Latency and error rate tracked per connection
  • Fixed: beta connections were missing from the search index
May 19, 2024
v2.3
NewEvidence

Hash-chained decision record

Every human and worker action is appended to a verifiable chain. Exports are signed with the workspace key and ready to use as control evidence.

  • 400-day retention with on-demand verification
  • Signed CSV export
  • Separate GDPR erasure log
